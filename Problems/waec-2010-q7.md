## Question
Solve $2x^2 - 5x + 3 = 0$

## Solution
Using the quadratic formula:
$$x = \frac{5 \pm \sqrt{25 - 24}}{4} = \frac{5 \pm 1}{4}$$

Therefore $x = \frac{3}{2}$ or $x = 1$
