Completing the square is a method used to solve quadratic equations and rewrite them in vertex form.

## Method
For $ax^2 + bx + c = 0$, rewrite as:
$$\left(x + \frac{b}{2a}\right)^2 = \frac{b^2 - 4ac}{4a^2}$$
